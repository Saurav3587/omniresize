Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

100% In-Browser Guarantee:

Your photos, documents, and signatures are processed exclusively in your device's browser memory (RAM) using HTML5 Canvas & WebAssembly. They are NEVER uploaded to any remote server.

1. Introduction

Welcome to OmniResize Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via https://omniresize.pro. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is handled, and your rights.

2. Image Processing & Data Transmission

Unlike traditional online image editors that transmit your private images to cloud data centers for processing, OmniResize operates entirely client-side:

  • No File Uploads: When you drag, drop, or select photos, they remain strictly inside your web browser.
  • No File Retention: We have no access to your images. No photos are stored, viewed, analyzed, or shared.
  • Automatic Metadata Stripping: Our compression engine automatically removes EXIF tags, GPS location coordinates, and camera device serials from your output images.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies to serve ads when you visit our website:

  • Google AdSense: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on OmniResize Studio.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the Internet.
  • Opt-Out Options: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Log Files & Web Analytics

Like most websites, we may collect standard, non-personally identifiable log information through Cloudflare Web Analytics, including browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), referring/exit pages, operating system platforms, date/time stamps, and aggregate visitor counts. This data is used solely to analyze performance trends, prevent abuse, and optimize website speed.

5. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

Under regulations like GDPR (European Economic Area) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), users have the right to request information about data handling. Because OmniResize does not store accounts, names, or uploaded photos on any server, we hold zero personal image files associated with any user.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@omniresize.pro.
