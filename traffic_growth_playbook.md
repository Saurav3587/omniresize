## 📋 Phase 1: Search Engine Submission & Instant Indexing (Day 1)

As soon as your site is live on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify with `omniresize.pro`:

### 1. Google Search Console (GSC)
1. Visit **[Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)**.
2. Add Property: Choose **Domain** and enter `omniresize.pro`.
3. Verification: Add the provided TXT record to your DNS (Cloudflare makes this instant).
4. **Submit Sitemap**:
   - Go to **Sitemaps** in the left sidebar.
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml` and hit **Submit**.
   - Status will show green **Success** with all 7 indexed URLs.
5. **Fast-Track Indexing (URL Inspection)**:
   - Paste each of your high-traffic URLs one-by-one into the top search bar:
     - `https://omniresize.pro/`
     - `https://omniresize.pro/resize-image-to-50kb`
     - `https://omniresize.pro/resize-image-to-20kb`
     - `https://omniresize.pro/resize-image-to-100kb`
     - `https://omniresize.pro/bulk-image-resizer`
     - `https://omniresize.pro/passport-photo-maker`
     - `https://omniresize.pro/crop-image`
   - Click **"Request Indexing"** on each. This forces Googlebot to crawl your pages within 24–48 hours instead of waiting weeks!

### 2. Bing Webmaster Tools & IndexNow
1. Go to **[Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters)**.
2. Click **"Import from Google Search Console"** (takes 10 seconds).
3. Bing powers search results for Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, and AI engines (ChatGPT search, Copilot).
