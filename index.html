Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about resizing, compressing, and editing photos on OmniResize.

How do I resize an image to under 50 KB or 20 KB?
Simply upload your photo to OmniResize, navigate to the Compress tab, and click the < 50 KB or < 20 KB quick chip. Our binary search compression algorithm will automatically calculate the exact JPEG/WebP quality level and dimension scaling to guarantee your file stays under the target size without manual trial and error.
Are my uploaded photos safe and confidential?
Yes, 100%. Conventional resizers upload your photos to external servers where they can be cached or logged. OmniResize runs entirely inside your browser's local sandbox using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Your photos never leave your device, making it 100% compliant for sensitive IDs, passport scans, signatures, and confidential documents.
Can I batch resize multiple images at the same time?
Yes! Click Bulk Resizer in the top menu or drag and drop a folder of images into the dropzone. You can apply uniform percentage scaling, max-fit bounding boxes, exact dimensions, or target file size limits across hundreds of images, then download all results in a single organized ZIP archive.
Will resizing blur or distort my image quality?
No. OmniResize uses multi-step bicubic resampling and optional unsharp-mask convolution filtering to preserve crisp lines and natural textures. For enlarging smaller photos, our 2× HD and 4× Ultra HD super-resolution upscalers enhance clarity without pixelation.
How do I format an image for 300 DPI passport or printing?
In the Resize tab, change the dimension unit from px to in (inches) or mm, enter your required physical size (e.g. 2×2 inches for US Passport, 35×45mm for EU/India), and set the DPI dropdown to 300 DPI. OmniResize injects an authentic JFIF resolution header so printers and photo studios detect the exact 300 DPI metadata.
